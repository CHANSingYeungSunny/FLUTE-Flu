## Corrections driven by reading

The original reference incorrectly called Kramer “A. M.”. The correct author is Sarah C. Kramer. MIFlu authors are Jaeuk Moon, Jonghwa Shim, Eunbeen Kim and Eenjun Hwang. The draft’s “only two baselines / paper single seed” claim conflicts with its own paper notes: the notes describe a larger Table V baseline set and ten repetitions. Remove it until the paper PDF and a pinned author revision settle the exact configuration. The author repository and its raw-file endpoint were inaccessible here; no upstream correction or its absence was verified.

The phrase “first demonstration that LLMs fail” is unsupported. Linear-baseline and LLM-removal critiques predate this project. The 2026 Qiu preprint presents counterevidence under different data regimes. This snapshot tests adapted implementations on one series, not all of the original algorithms or all foundation models.
