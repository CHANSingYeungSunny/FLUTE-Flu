# Standing policy for each new result handoff

The owner authorises a larger scientific rewrite whenever more results are supplied. First verify archive hashes, raw predictions, per-run configs, completeness, scoring panels and statistical units. Preserve earlier result snapshots. Then revise every affected method, table, figure, abstract, result, limitation and conclusion in the internal reports, FYP progress reports, IEEE manuscript, journal records, README, presentation, bilingual talking notes and Q&A together. Retain Q01–Q25 and named-paper reasoning in the separate Chinese Q&A companion (01_最终报告/report_internal_qa_zh.docx / .pdf). Keep the main Chinese internal report concise, without embedded question/answer blocks. Update both documents when evidence changes their claims; preserve question identities and literature reasoning, not obsolete numerical answers. Do not force new outcomes to match the old rankings or p-values. Regenerate, render, inspect and deliver the integrated DOCX/PDF/PPTX corpus with the source and an evidence-keyed change log.

A new result handoff is the trigger. This does not create unattended monitoring or an assumption that HPC jobs have finished. No publication acceptance or clinical deployment claim is inferred from job completion. Actual unexecuted checks remain labelled with commands.
